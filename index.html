Tips & Essentials

Practical notes for the trip
Driving in Japan
International Driving Permit (IDP) is mandatory — obtain at AA South Africa before departure. Drive on the left (same as SA). ETC card useful for toll roads. Hokkaido and Miyakojima are both easy, low-traffic driving.
Koyasan · Fudo-in
Check-in strictly 15:00–17:00. From Wakayama, leave by noon. Dinner 17:30–19:00. Public bath 16:30–20:30 only. Morning ceremony 07:00 — don't skip it. Portions at dinner are light — bring snacks for Maya. No gluten-free option.
Gion Matsuri · Kyoto
Main procession (Yamahoko Junko) starts 9am on 18 July. Streets close from 18:00 on eve (17 Jul). Can board floats ¥500–1,000. Historic houses open artworks to public (Byobu Matsuri).
Kikunoi Honten · 17 July 19:00
Confirmed 17 July at 19:00. Smart casual — no shorts or sandals. Allow 2.5–3 hours, finishing ~21:30. Take taxi from Gion (~15 min, ~¥1,500). Walk Ninenzaka & Sannenzaka lantern-lit lanes after dinner. Note strict cancellation policy.
Nakashima · Hiroshima
Set menu only at ¥20,790pp. 3 Michelin stars. Chef comes out for photos after service. Book direct — no known surcharge platform. Reservations open ~2 months ahead.
Miyajima Tides
The floating torii gate illusion works best at high tide. Check tide times at willyweather.com or similar before your visit and plan accordingly.
Shakotan · Sea Urchin
Fishing ban lifts in June — July is peak season. The drive along Route 229 is spectacular — pull over at viewpoints along the coast.
Kyoto → Hiroshima · Shinkansen
19 July · ~15:00–15:30 departure · 1h40 · Sit left side seats A/B for Seto Inland Sea views around Okayama. Car 7, rows 3–10. Buy an ekiben (station bento) at Kyoto Station before boarding — a quintessential Shinkansen experience.
Hiroshima → Tokyo · Overnight Bus
~12 hours · Book premium or semi-premium seat (2+1 configuration) for comfort. Book early — July peak fills fast. Book at willer-travel.com/en/ or kosokubus.com/en/
Tokyo · Narita Departure
ET673 departs Narita at 20h40 on 23 July. Take subway from Asakusa to Ueno (1 stop), then Narita Express (N'EX) — about 60 min total. Allow 3 hours before departure, so leave hotel by 17h00. Booking.com airport transfer also booked.
