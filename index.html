🏯
Must See · Morning
Gyeongbokgung Palace & Bukchon Hanok Village
Gyeongbokgung: arrive before 9am — changing of the guard ceremony at 10am and 14:00 (free, spectacular). The palace grounds cover 410,000m² with restored throne halls, gardens and pavilions. Allow 2 hours · Bukchon: the upper village (north of Anguk station) has the best-preserved hanok lanes — 30-minute walk from Gyeongbokgung. Go before 10am before tour groups arrive. The lower village near Samcheong-dong is more café-lined but equally charming
🥢
Food & Culture
Gwangjang Market · Seoul's oldest covered market
The food stalls here are legendary — bindaetteok (mung bean pancakes, crispy, served with makgeolli), yukhoe (raw beef tartare with egg yolk and Asian pear), mayak gimbap (bite-sized seaweed rice rolls the size of your thumb, addictive). Sit at the stall counters, not the restaurant section. Open until late. The textile market surrounds it for fabric and traditional crafts
🛍️
Shopping & Street Food
Myeongdong & Insadong
Myeongdong: Korea's K-beauty capital — Innisfree, Etude House, Laneige, Missha all have flagship stores. The street food vendors at night are exceptional: egg bread, hotteok, tornado potatoes, corn dogs. Very tourist-heavy but fun · Insadong: artisan craft shops, traditional tea houses, hanji (Korean paper) goods. More atmosphere, less commercial than Myeongdong · Ikseon-dong alley (5 min walk from Insadong): converted early 20th century Korean houses now housing tiny cocktail bars and coffee shops — the coolest evening drinking spot in Seoul
🏯
Maya's Pick · Quieter Royal Palace
Changgyeonggung Palace — less visited, more intimate
Connected to Changdeokgung, this palace is far less visited than Gyeongbokgung — you may have courtyards almost to yourself. Built 1484. The pond and botanical garden is beautiful in July. ₩1,000 entry · Compare: Gyeongbokgung is grandest and most restored, Changdeokgung has the famous Secret Garden (book ahead), Changgyeonggung is most peaceful and local-feeling
🛕
Maya's Pick · Buddhist Temple
Jogyesa Temple — Korea's most important Buddhist temple
Right in central Seoul, 5 min from Anguk station. The main hall (Daeungjeon) is one of Korea's largest wooden structures. Free entry, open all day. The lantern decorations are spectacular — Seoul's Buddhist heartbeat. Surrounding streets full of Buddhist craft shops and tea houses
🌿
Maya's Pick · Hilltop Park
Naksan Park — Seoul City Wall views
Forested hilltop on the eastern edge of Bukchon where you can walk the original Seoul City Wall. Views over old-city rooftops are spectacular. Almost entirely visited by locals. 20-min walk from Ikseon-dong. Best at dusk for golden light over hanok rooftops
⛰️
Maya's Pick · Mountain Temple
Jingwansa Temple — Bukhansan slopes
Ancient temple on Bukhansan National Park mountain slopes. Take subway to Gupabal station then bus — the cedar forest approach walk is half the experience. Atmospheric in morning mist. Combine with a short hike up the mountain for city panoramas
⛰️
Maya's Pick · Shamanist Mountain
Inwangsan Mountain Park — Seoul fortress walls + shamanism
Rocky western mountain famous for fortress walls and shamanist shrines. The Guksadang shrine near the summit is one of the last active shamanist shrines in Seoul — you may see shamans performing gut (ritual ceremonies). Dramatic rock formations, excellent city views. 40-min hike from base
🌾
Maya's Pick · Haneul Park
Haneul Park — hilltop with windmills and Han River views
Elevated park on old landfill with five windmills, green grass plateau and panoramic views of the Han River and city. 291 steps to the top (lift available). Best at sunset. Combine with the World Cup Stadium area nearby
🛤️
Maya's Pick · Hidden Alley
Seosulla-gil — palace wall street
Quiet atmospheric alley along the western wall of Gyeongbokgung — stone palace walls on one side, traditional houses and small galleries on the other. Very local. Perfect slow afternoon walk before visiting the palace
🐟
Maya's Markets · 노량진수산시장
Noryangjin Fish Market — live seafood, pick and eat
Open 24/7 — tanks of live fish, crabs, shellfish, sea urchin. Pick your catch downstairs, take it upstairs to a restaurant for preparation. Flounder sashimi for 2–3pp costs ₩30,000–50,000. Try sannakji (live wriggling octopus). Ask for maeuntang (spicy fish soup from bones) at the end — usually free. Best times: evening 18:00+ (busy and atmospheric) or 03:00 for the live fish auction (chaotic, extraordinary). Caution: some upstairs restaurants inflate prices for tourists — check menu prices before ordering and confirm the cooking fee upfront
🥩
Maya's Markets · 마장 축산물시장
Majang Meat Market — Korean beef at source
Korea's largest meat market, supplying 60% of the country's beef. The experience: browse butchers, select Hanwoo (1++ grade is highest, comparable to Wagyu A5) at 20–30% below restaurant prices, pay ~₩6,000pp grilling fee upstairs and BBQ over charcoal. Ask for chuck flap tail or short plate. Bring a bag for your jacket (smoke). Best 11am–8pm
🍩
Maya's Markets · 망원시장
Mangwon Market — Seoul's favourite local market
Neighbourhood favourite near Hongdae, completely untouristy. Famous handmade twisted donuts, fish cakes, gimbap, tteokbokki from family stalls. Relaxed snack-walk vibe rather than sit-down meal. Weekdays quieter. Easy to combine with Hongdae for an evening out. Hours: 10am–9pm, many closed Monday
🛍️
Maya's Markets · 남대문시장
Namdaemun Market — Seoul's oldest market, since 1414
10,000+ shops in covered arcades — clothing, accessories, kitchenware, traditional goods, souvenirs. Less touristy than Gwangjang. Street food: hotteok (sweet pancakes), odeng (fish cake skewers), bindaetteok. Some stalls from 5am. 10-min walk from Nine Tree — ideal for last-minute shopping before Tokyo · Market comparison summary: Gwangjang = food theatre. Noryangjin = pick-and-eat seafood adventure. Majang = premium beef BBQ. Mangwon = local neighbourhood bites. Namdaemun = browse + street food
🍜
Maya's Pick · Restaurant
Samcheongdong Sujebi — hand-torn noodle soup
Famous sujebi restaurant in the charming Samcheongdong neighbourhood between Gyeongbokgung and Bukchon. Hand-torn wheat flour noodles in anchovy broth — simple, deeply satisfying. Almost always a queue but moves fast. Perfect lunch stop after Bukchon or Gyeongbokgung
🍲
Maya's Pick · Restaurant
Oegojip Seoulleongtang — ox bone soup institution
A Seoul institution serving seolleongtang (milky white ox bone broth soup with thin noodles and tender beef) since the 1970s. Open 24 hours. The soup is mild and restorative — season yourself at the table with salt and spring onions. A quintessential Seoul working-class meal
🌇
Evening
Namsan Seoul Tower at dusk
Take the cable car from Myeongdong (5 min walk) — departs every few minutes, ¥9,000 return. The observation deck at 237m gives 360° views of the entire Seoul basin. Go 30 minutes before sunset — watch the city transform from day to night. The love locks on the fence are iconic. Alternatively hike up through Namsan Park (40 min, beautiful forested path) and take cable car back down. Best photo spot is the terrace on the south side for the Han River in the background

Deep House / Progressive House / Techno
Faust · Itaewon · RA #1 in Seoul
Three floors, consistently books serious international artists — Boris, nthng, Cryptofauna. Resident DJs Mars Parck and Suman are exceptional. Dark, serious, music-first crowd. The best club in Seoul full stop. Check ra.co/clubs/106806 for lineup.
BBCB (Beton Brut + Concrete Bar) · Itaewon · RA #2
Two rooms in one basement: Beton Brut for heavier techno and high-energy house, Concrete Bar for deeper minimal sounds. Celebrating 9 years — one of Seoul's most respected underground institutions. ra.co/clubs/127189
Cakeshop · Itaewon · RA #4
Warehouse-style basement, genre-spanning — house, techno, bass, experimental. Has hosted Machine Girl, Bianca Oblivion, Shannen SP. Their in-house label Carousel Records is strong. Runs own Boiler Room sessions. Thu–Sun.
vurt. · Mapo · RA #5
Small concrete bunker in Hapjeong, focused on underground techno and minimal house. Open 3–4 times per month, quality over quantity. Local and international bookings — Tokyo acts visit regularly. ra.co/clubs/101673
Modeci · Hongdae · Funktion One
5th floor with rooftop city views, Funktion One sound system, deep house focus. More accessible vibe than Faust/BBCB. Active on RA. ra.co/clubs/140937
Hip Hop / Funk / R&B
The Henz Club · Hongdae
The underground hip hop institution in Seoul — Funktion One system, resident crews Deadend Movement and 360sounds. Active on RA. Fashion-meets-music crowd. ra.co/clubs/128746
Pistil · Itaewon · Cakeshop sister venue
Mellower than Cakeshop — deeper sounds, better food, Thursdays often free. Good starting point if you want to warm up before heading to Faust. ra.co/clubs/104589
How to plan: Check ra.co/events/kr/seoul for exact lineups in early July — DJs are everything. Cover ₩10,000–25,000. Last subway before midnight — stay till 5am first train or take a taxi. Itaewon is more international, serious music crowd. Hongdae is younger, cheaper, more energetic. Both 20 min from Nine Tree by taxi.

Tokyo Day 1
▾
🏘️
Neighbourhood · Morning
Yanaka · Old Tokyo preserved
One of the few Tokyo neighbourhoods that survived both the 1923 earthquake and WWII bombing — feels like Tokyo 100 years ago. Walk Yanaka Ginza shopping street (covered arcade selling traditional snacks, crafts, cat-themed everything). The Yanaka Cemetery is genuinely lovely for a stroll — cherry trees, old stone graves, cats everywhere. Best visited early morning before it gets hot. Start at Nippori station and wander south toward Nezu shrine (one of Tokyo's oldest, vermillion torii gates, no crowds)
🐟
Food · Morning
Tsukiji Outer Market · Tokyo's greatest breakfast
The inner wholesale market moved to Toyosu in 2018, but the outer market (where all the food stalls are) is very much alive. Go 7:30–9:00am before tour groups arrive. Must-eat: fresh tuna rice bowl (maguro don) at a counter stall — look for queues of Japanese people, not foreigners. Tamagoyaki (rectangular sweet egg omelette) on a stick. Uni (sea urchin) if you haven't had enough in Hokkaido. Oysters. Fresh sushi at 8am is genuinely one of life's great experiences. Budget ¥1,500–3,000pp for a proper breakfast feast
🎋
Neighbourhood
Harajuku · Two very different streets
Takeshita-dori: covered pedestrian street of wild teen fashion, crepes, overloaded bubble tea. Chaotic, fun, completely unlike anywhere else. Best after 11am when it gets busy · Omotesando (2 min walk from Takeshita): the exact opposite — wide tree-lined boulevard of high-end international flagships (Prada, Louis Vuitton, Comme des Garçons) designed by Tadao Ando and other architects. The buildings themselves are worth seeing. The side streets (Ura-Harajuku) between these two worlds have the best independent fashion and coffee
⛩️
Morning · Best early
Meiji Shrine · Japan's most important Shinto shrine
Dedicated to Emperor Meiji and Empress Shoken. The forested approach (700m of gravel path lined with 120,000 trees) is serene and one of the great walks in Tokyo. Arrive before 8am to see morning prayers and occasionally traditional wedding ceremonies (Saturdays especially). Free entry. The iris garden nearby is beautiful in July. Takes 45 min to do properly
🗻
Day Trip · If Tokyo is Too Hot — Hakone
Hakone — mountain air, Mt Fuji, volcanic valley
July in Tokyo can hit 35°C+ with high humidity. Hakone sits at elevation and is significantly cooler — a genuine escape. 85 minutes from Shinjuku on the Romancecar express (¥2,470, book at odakyu.jp, no JR Pass). Key stops: Owakudani volcanic valley — sulphur vents, lunar landscape, famous black eggs (kurotamago — cooked in volcanic springs, ¥600 for 5, legend says each adds 7 years to your life). Lake Ashi — iconic view of Mt Fuji across the lake (cloud permitting in July — check the morning before you go). Hakone Open Air Museum — outdoor sculpture park with a Picasso pavilion, genuinely world-class. A full Hakone day trip uses the Hakone Free Pass (¥6,100 from Shinjuku, covers all transport within Hakone). Depart Shinjuku by 9am, back by 19:00. Worth it if the city heat is unbearable
🏛️
Free Views · Tokyo
Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building · Best free observation deck
202m twin towers with free observation decks on the 45th floor of both North and South towers. Open until 23:00 (South) and 22:30 (North) — spectacular night views. North tower is particularly impressive. Far less crowded than Tokyo Skytree or Tokyo Tower, and completely free. 5 min walk from Shinjuku Station west exit — perfect to combine with Golden Gai and Omoide Yokocho on the same evening
🌿
Garden · Best in Tokyo
Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden · Three gardens in one
Tokyo's finest formal garden — 58.3 hectares combining a French formal garden, English landscape garden, and traditional Japanese garden in one space. The greenhouse has tropical plants. In July the summer flowers and greenery are at their best. ¥500 entry. Open 09:00–18:00. The best escape from urban Tokyo — inside the garden the city noise drops away completely. Note: no alcohol permitted (rare for Japan)
🍶
Evening
Shinjuku — Golden Gai & Omoide Yokocho
Golden Gai: 200+ tiny bars each fitting around 6 people — pick a bar by peering in the window, pay a small cover charge, talk to whoever is there. Omoide Yokocho: smoke-filled yakitori alley under the Shinjuku tracks, beer and grilled chicken, unchanged since the 1950s. Both within 5 min walk of Shinjuku Station east exit

Tokyo Clubs — Shibuya & Aoyama
▾
Deep House / Progressive House
VENT · Aoyama · RA top pick
The definitive deep house club in Tokyo — only real club in Aoyama, extraordinary hi-res sound system, carefully chosen bookings (Special Request, Dubfire, HAAI, D.TIFFANY). Doubles as a gallery by day. Fri/Sat 11pm–5am · 2 min from Omotesando station · ra.co/clubs/122892
ENTER · Shibuya · Successor to Contact (closed 2022)
Opened December 2022 by the former Contact team with the same Meyer Sound system. 150-capacity, DJ booth in the centre of the floor for 360° sound. Sits near Miyashita Park, Shibuya. Underground house and techno, emerging Japanese and international artists. Sister to DJ Bar Bridge and Wrep — wristband gets you into all. ra.co for lineup.
WOMB · Shibuya · The big room option
Tokyo's longest-running serious club, 1,000-cap, recently renovated. Four floors including hip hop. When WOMB books well (Carl Cox, DJ Nobu, Peggy Gou) it's exceptional. Check the lineup — it varies enormously. ra.co/clubs/54252 (listed as WWW on RA)
Hip Hop / Funk / Soul
DJ Bar Koara · Shibuya backstreets
Tiny basement bar, ¥1,000 entry, rotating DJs playing Techno/House/Disco/Funk/Soul. Monthly Disco Mania night is legendary. The most unassuming and consistently excellent small venue in Tokyo.
Aoyama Hachi · Aoyama · voted best Tokyo club
30th anniversary in 2025, Time Out Tokyo award. Four floors in a narrow building, each with different music — hip hop, house, rock. View over Roppongi-dori from the 2nd floor. Open till 5am. RA-listed.
Key: Contact is permanently closed (demolished 2022). Sound Museum Vision also closed. ENTER is their replacement. Always check ra.co/events/jp/tokyo before committing — the DJ is everything. Last train ~midnight from Shibuya, then taxi back to Hotel Niwa. Clubs run till 5am.

🌊
Must Do · Cape Kamui
Cape Kamui · The tip of Shakotan
Walk from the trailhead to the cape tip is 20 minutes each way — moderate but exposed. The rock at the very end gives 270° views of the Japan Sea with the Shakotan Blue water below. Sea caves visible from the clifftop. In July the wild flowers are out. Go early morning before tour buses arrive. The colour of the water is genuinely unlike anything in mainland Japan — iridescent turquoise caused by the depth and clarity · There's a small shrine at the cape tip where fishermen pray before going out to sea
🏊
Swimming
Shimamui Beach · Best beach on the peninsula
A 20-minute drive from Cape Kamui — rocky arch frames a small beach with crystal clear water. July sea temperature is around 20°C — cold but swimmable. The coastal rock formations are spectacular. Much less visited than the cape itself. Bring snorkelling gear if you have it — visibility is extraordinary
🚗
Drive Tips
Route 229 · Shakotan Peninsula coastal road
The coastal road hugging the peninsula is one of Hokkaido's most beautiful drives. Stops worth making: Yobetsu fishing port (small, authentic, fishermen mending nets in the morning), Kamui Misaki Lighthouse viewpoint, the rock formations at Ogon-hama. Total lap of the peninsula takes about 2.5 hrs at a leisurely pace with stops. Petrol station in Bikuni — fill up there as there's nothing at the cape end
