Tips & Essentials

Practical notes for the trip
Koyasan · Fudo-in
Check-in strictly 15:00–17:00. From Wakayama, leave by noon. Dinner 17:30–19:00. Public bath 16:30–20:30 only. Morning ceremony 07:00 — don't skip it. Portions at dinner are light — bring snacks for Maya. No gluten-free option.
Gion Matsuri · Kyoto
Main procession (Yamahoko Junko) starts 9am on 17 July. Streets close from 18:00 on eve (16 Jul). Can board floats ¥500–1,000. Historic houses open artworks to public (Byobu Matsuri). Kyoto is 15 min from Osaka by Shinkansen.
Kikunoi Honten · 17 July 19:00
Confirmed 17 July at 19:00. Smart casual — no shorts or sandals. Allow 2.5–3 hours, finishing ~21:30. Take taxi from Gion (~15 min, ~¥1,500). Walk Ninenzaka & Sannenzaka lantern-lit lanes after dinner. Note cancellation policy — significant fee for late cancellations.
Nakashima · Hiroshima
Reservations open approximately 2 months ahead. Set menu only at ¥20,790pp. 3 Michelin stars. Chef comes out for photos after service. Book direct — no known surcharge platform.
Driving in Japan
International Driving Permit (IDP) is mandatory — obtain at AA South Africa before departure. Drive on the left (same as SA). ETC card useful for toll roads. Hokkaido and Miyakojima are both easy, low-traffic driving.
Miyajima Tides
The floating torii gate illusion works best at high tide. Check tide times at willyweather.com or similar before your visit and plan accordingly.
Shakotan · Sea Urchin
Fishing ban lifts in June — July is peak season. Ask your inn about the uni don lunch at Rinkousou nearby. The drive along Route 229 is spectacular — pull over at viewpoints along the coast.
Tokyo · Narita
ET673 departs Narita at 20h40 on 23 July. Take subway from Asakusa to Ueno (1 stop), then Narita Express (N'EX) — about 60 min total. Allow 3 hours before departure, so leave hotel by 17h00. Booking.com airport transfer also booked.
